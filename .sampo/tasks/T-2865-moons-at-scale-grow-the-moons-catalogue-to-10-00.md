# Moons at scale: grow the Moons catalogue to 10,000 candidate moons about what Sampo can do for its user, by grid, through a cheap-first filter funnel, clustered into capability families, with the climb on families and a Moons page of families and funnel counts
## Raw request
> Moons at scale: grow the Moons catalogue to 10,000 candidate moons about what Sampo can do for its user, by grid, through a cheap-first filter funnel, clustered into capability families, with the climb on families and a Moons page of families and funnel counts
## Interpretation
Reading 1 (chosen): a standing pipeline, not a one-off batch. Candidates are generated by grid into their own store under the tower (research/genesis/moons/candidates.jsonl, with an append-only funnel log of each one's fate and reason), separate from moonshot.md: 10,000 blocks would drown the curated catalogue and its cold reads. Each candidate carries its grid cell and the line 'Today you can't ___. With this, you can ___.', and the write rejects internal-only payoffs and missing or unfalsifiable kill tests. A cheap-first funnel (dedupe by embedding, a one-day kill test check, a cheap tier screen, novelty) leaves survivors that cluster into families. Only family leads get the strongest model's cold tier and kill-test design, and they are promoted into moonshot.md as M-NN, so the existing rate, climb and floor machinery runs on families. The tower export and page show families and funnel counts. Reading 2 (ruled out): append 10,000 blocks to moonshot.md and rate them all; the strongest model on 10,000 ideas breaks the steer's own cost rule, and the tower doc's cold read can't read that file.
