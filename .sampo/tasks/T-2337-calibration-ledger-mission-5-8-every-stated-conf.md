> Calibration ledger (Mission 5, #8): every stated confidence is a write-once bet scored later by signals Foreman didn't author, beside a base-rate forecast; fm calibration shows buckets and Brier, and the research and race bars move from it only as registered trials
## Interpretation
Reading 1: a ledger that records each stated number when it is stated and scores it later against what happened, judged by outcome survival (reverts, re-fixes, corrections within 30 days), which seed rule 5 counts as signals Foreman didn't author. Reading 2: the existing fmoutcomes.calibration (a task's confidence=N against holding on the first finish) is enough once briefs state a number. 0 of 2332 briefs ever set confidence, and Reading 2 can't score choices, assumptions, decisions or races: Reading 1, with Reading 2 folded in.
## Assumptions (confidence)
## Acceptance criteria
- [x] a stated confidence on a brief's choice or an assumption (at first focus), a decision (fm decide --confidence N) or a race side writes one row to the project's calibration.jsonl (kind, stated, base rate, task, files, tree); rows are append-only (an outcome is a new row; a stated value can't be edited); the Brier that can raise a bar uses only fm outcomes' 30-day re-fixes and reverts, signals Foreman didn't author; race results, assumption marks and reversed decisions are recorded beside it as Foreman's own evidence and never move a bar — verify with `python3 plugin/tests/run.py test_outcomes -k CalibrationLedger`
- [x] fm calibration prints per-kind buckets (n, held, gap), the Brier of the stated numbers beside the base rate's (the held-on-first-finish share of earlier closes of the same tier and type from fm outcomes, all closes while a pair has under 20), 'too few' under 20 rows, the research and race bars, and what each bar cost (races and research it triggered); the bar check runs at each fm task finish (where closes are already recorded): with 20+ scored rows in a bucket overconfident by 15+ points it raises the bar at most 10 points (at most 90), as an fm decide row and a registered trial with Brier as its metric; Foreman never lowers a bar: an underconfident bar, or stated numbers no better than the base rate, are reported and fm decide --review carries them to the final report for the user; fm digest and fm cost read the ledger, whose task rows score the same outcomes fmoutcomes.calibration did, so that function is removed — verify with `python3 plugin/tests/run.py test_outcomes -k CalibrationBars`
## Non-goals
## Approach (options → choice → why)
1) one append-only calibration.jsonl, a base-rate forecast beside every row, scored by fm outcomes, races, assumption marks and reversals; fails if: rows sit unscored while outcomes lag 30 days, so they show as pending, never as held. 2) use what exists: keep fmoutcomes.calibration and require confidence=N at focus; fails if: one axis, no choices, assumptions or decisions. 3) do less: report only, no bar moves; fails if: overconfidence never changes what Foreman does, so 'researches when unsure' stays a feeling. 4) one store per feature (decisions.md tags, brief meta, race meta); fails if: five readers and no single Brier. Choice 1 (75%): one ledger serves #8's 'states confidence' and the bars briefs 4 and 5 read; the base rate (about 79% held, per Mission 4's reopened-ticket miner) is what a stated number has to beat.
