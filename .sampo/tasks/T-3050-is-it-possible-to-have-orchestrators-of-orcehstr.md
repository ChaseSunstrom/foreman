# Kalevala: many Rune towers at once on every aspect of Sampo, overseen by Väinämöinen, and evolving its own setup
## Raw request
> is it possible to have orchestrators of orcehstrators, so we can have something like a genesis of genesis run, where we have a genesis tower managing multiple other simulatinous genesis towers? and also, many different aspects of Sampo, not just a few things too? etc. and the ability for a super orchestrator to monitor how theyre doing and keep them on track with what the user wanted and such?
## Interpretation
Reading 1 (taken): an orchestrator of orchestrators. Kalevala (the epic that gathers many runes) runs many Rune towers at once, one per aspect of Sampo (problem solving, memory, efficiency, verification, orchestration, models, moons, UI and every coverage.md area, chosen from gap data, not a short hand list); Väinämöinen (the singer who keeps the song true) watches every tower's progress, exam deltas, drift, stalls and spend against what the user wanted (gen-0, the standing intent, the steers, the mandate) and acts: re-steers a tower's next seed, pauses or restarts one, moves slots to towers that improve, merges duplicates, opens towers for empty aspects, and reports; and Kalevala has its own generations, so the tower set and how towers are set up evolve, selected by the towers' combined exam results (genesis of genesis). Reading 2 (ruled out): one tower with more Missions: it already has Missions per area; the ask is simultaneous towers with their own selection and a supervisor. Reading 3 (later): towers across different projects: one project's towers first, the design keeps a tower's project explicit so another project's tower can join.
## Assumptions (confidence)
Each tower is the existing Rune machinery under its own home (research/genesis/towers/NAME) with a gen-0 derived from the root gen-0 plus its aspect, sealed the same way (90%); the moons store, the exam scenarios and the steers stay shared (85%); local models carry the bulk and Claude the cold reads, under the budget (no weekly lean on this project, T-3049) (85%); towers' code changes go through lanes and the usual gates, with a file-overlap check between towers (80%).
## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Building on built things: every closed task that changes how Sampo behaves (how it comes up with ideas, thinks, reviews, plans, remembers) is registered as a capability, and Kalevala's next generation puts it into every tower's seed and measures it there, so each gain compounds; Kalevala never stops on its own (an infinite loop under the mandate, paused only by the user, a real limit or Väinämöinen's drift stop) — verify with `python3 plugin/tests/run.py test_kalevala`
- [ ] Orchestrators of orchestrators: Kalevala runs many Rune towers at once (genesis of genesis), each with its own home, gen-0 (the root's plus its aspect, sealed), lineage and exam slice, steps running simultaneously as jobs — verify with `python3 plugin/tests/run.py test_kalevala`
- [ ] Many different aspects of Sampo, not just a few: a tower per aspect with open coverage (every coverage.md area plus problem solving, memory and efficiency), chosen from gap data, more opened as cells empty — verify with `python3 plugin/tests/run.py test_kalevala`
- [ ] A super orchestrator monitors how they're doing and keeps them on track with what the user wanted: Väinämöinen reads each tower's progress, exam deltas, drift, stalls and spend against gen-0, the standing intent and the steers, and acts (re-steer a seed, pause, restart, move slots, merge duplicates) with each act logged and reported — verify with `python3 plugin/tests/run.py test_kalevala`
- [ ] A cooler name than genesis of genesis: Kalevala and Väinämöinen in the CLI, help, MASTER.md and the live page, which shows every tower's progress — verify with `python3 plugin/tests/run.py test_kalevala test_live`
- [ ] A hard spend ceiling under the never-ending loop: Kalevala has its own budget feature and a daily share of the day's cap; local models first; a round refuses to start once its share is spent and the loop waits for the next day; at most K towers active at once (K from the local slots and the budget), the rest queued thinnest aspect first — verify with `python3 plugin/tests/run.py test_kalevala`
- [ ] Väinämöinen's acts are reversible and watched: restart and merge keep every generation folder (a snapshot before the act, an undo line in the act's ledger row); 'merge duplicates' pauses one tower and points it at the other, deleting nothing; its own steers pass the same drift check against gen-0 before they're written, and Kalevala's generation gets a cold read of its acts — verify with `python3 plugin/tests/run.py test_kalevala`
- [ ] Towers share safely: writes to the shared moons store go through its lock, exam scenarios are read-only to towers, each tower's exam lines are its own; two towers' steps writing the same file at once is refused (tested with two concurrent steps) — verify with `python3 plugin/tests/run.py test_kalevala`
- [ ] Proven live, not only by unit tests: a real Kalevala round on this project runs at least 4 towers through 2 generations each; a planted off-intent line in one tower's seed is caught by Väinämöinen and steered back, and a planted stall is restarted; both shown in sampo kalevala status and the ledger — verify with `python3 plugin/tests/run.py test_kalevala_live`
## Non-goals
Unbounded recursion (depth is Kalevala over towers, two levels; a deeper level only after the two prove out); rewriting any tower's or the root's MUSTs and NEVERs (drift refuses it); towers on sensitive projects sending records to the cloud.
## Approach (options → choice → why)
1) use what exists: namespaced towers in this project (rune.home honours a tower name, one shared root for moons, exam and steers) and a Kalevala loop that runs sampo genesis --tower NAME verbs as jobs: likely outcome: N towers on the machinery already proven over 4 generations. 2) do less: one tower with more Missions: fails if: no per-aspect selection, nothing runs at once, no supervisor. 3) a full project (worktree) per tower: fails if: merging towers' state and code back is heavy and the moons/exam stores split. 4) recursion of any depth (a Mission may be a tower): fails if: unbounded spend and drift; kept as Kalevala's later generation question. Choice 1 (75%), Väinämöinen as a verb over the towers' files and ledger rows, Kalevala's own lineage in research/genesis/kalevala.
## Risks and rollback
Runaway spend: each tower has a slot and a budget share, Väinämöinen pauses a tower whose exam doesn't move in two generations; collisions: a Mission whose files another tower's open Mission holds waits; drift: every tower's seed passes drift against its own gen-0 and the root's MUSTs; rollback: towers live under research/genesis/towers (state), the root tower is untouched, the code change is reverted per child.
