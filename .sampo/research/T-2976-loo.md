Run: `sampo exam models --loo --limit 200` (bench run m20261104-110143603), 1,260 rows in research/models/bench.jsonl.

**Method**
- Each of 8 projects with closed tasks in the lake is held out whole in turn; 148 pooled held-out requests per use.
- Contestants:
  - the train majority;
  - BM25 k-NN;
  - every local decision backend: kev 0.8b/4b/9b, tev1:4b, d3:4b, and Jeff with its triage and code-router adapters.
- Each is scored at 7 abstain thresholds.
- The floor is a bootstrap over held-out projects (seed 2976, 5th percentile) of the per-project gain over the baseline, with at least 3 projects and no project worse.

**Verdicts**
- **intake-type: kev:4b-q8_0 at confidence ≥ 0.7 passes the floor.** Its lower bound is +0.013 over the train majority, across 8 held-out projects. This run was measure-only (no `--adopt`), so nothing went live; the next `--loo --adopt` run writes it.
- **intake-tier: nothing passes.** The best lower bound is +0.000 against the majority.
