Planning

Every request becomes a plan first

A plain request is enough. Foreman classifies it, writes a brief with what done means and the command that proves it, and only then lets an edit happen.

What a brief holds

  • Type and size. FIX, FEATURE, CLEAN, PERFORMANCE, SECURITY or RESEARCH, and S, M or L.
  • Acceptance criteria, each with the command that proves it.
  • Steps, scope and risks. M and L work lists its approaches first and says why it chose one.
  • An approval when the work is large, unless you put Foreman in full auto.
FIX: login times out after 30s on slow networks
FEATURE: export report as CSV @src/reports
CONTEXT: Django app; don't touch migrations
DONE-WHEN: all tests pass and the CSV opens in Excel

One task at a time

One task is active. New ideas land in the inbox instead of being chased halfway, and the queue orders itself: baseline, cleanups, performance, security, fixes, then features, dependencies first. NOW: switches on purpose.
