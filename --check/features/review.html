Audits and review

Independent eyes before anything is done

Finished work is checked through lenses with different prompts and context: intent, adversary, edge cases, operations and maintainability. Small tasks get a self-check; large ones get all five.

Review that learns

Every confirmed finding is logged with its lens and severity. The defect classes a project keeps shipping go into the next builder's brief, so the same mistake is checked for before it is made again. /foreman:review runs one reviewer per lens over a diff, then two skeptics try to refute each finding.
