# Moonshot Mission each generation from gen 2 (steer 2026-10-30 #5): fm genesis seed N (N…
## Raw request
> FEATURE!: Moonshot Mission each generation from gen 2 (steer 2026-10-30 #5): fm genesis seed N (N ≥ 2) writes one moonshot Mission for ideas that fit no coverage.md cell and no gen-0 §2 capability, judged only on novelty and value. Its pack adds (1) a far-domain lens (mechanisms borrowed from biology, economics, markets, immune systems, operating systems, compilers and science, translated into the harness); (2) a frontier digest from fm landscape and fm research ask, refreshed every 3rd generation, no repo sweeps; (3) a novelty score per idea (prior art found or not, how much better ours is) with a cold fm second read marking obvious ideas. Every moonshot is spiked; a failure goes to not-yet.md, never dropped. The §8 report lists the moonshots that shipped and which the user would never have asked for
## Interpretation
Steer 2026-10-30 #5, a standing rule from gen 2 on. The coverage grid and the 13 capabilities steer every Mission toward known shapes; one Mission per generation is kept for ideas that fit no cell and no capability, judged only on novelty and value. fm genesis seed N (N ≥ 2) writes that moonshot Mission's row and pack: a far-domain lens (mechanisms from biology, economics, markets, immune systems, operating systems, compilers and science, translated into the harness), a frontier digest from fm landscape and fm research ask kept in the tower and refreshed every 3rd generation (no repo sweeps, per gen-0's NEVER), and a novelty score per idea (prior art found or not, how much better ours is) with a cold fm second read that marks obvious ideas. Every moonshot is spiked; a failed one goes to not-yet.md with its blocker, never dropped. The §8 report lists the moonshots that shipped and which ones the user would never have asked for. The 8-Mission cap stays: the moonshot takes one of the eight.

## Non-goals
## Approach (options → choice → why)
Revised after fm second plan (verdict revise: obvious/novelty semantics and the cold read weren't checkable). Steer #5 marks obvious ideas, it doesn't drop them. Choices: (1) novelty: each moonshot idea carries prior-art and better-by lines and a 1-5 score; the obvious mark comes only from a cold read the verb runs itself (fm genesis novelty N, one cheap child, the writer never sees its prompt), stored in state (genesis-novelty.json, written through fm only, guard-protected like genesis-accepted.json) keyed by the idea's hash, so a mark typed into the file counts for nothing; an obvious idea stays listed and marked, and can't be the generation's spiked pick while a non-obvious one exists. (2) never asked for: computed, not judged: the report matches each shipped moonshot's distinctive words against the raw requests of user-sourced briefs and steers, printing 'asked: no' or the brief that asked it. (3) frontier: fm landscape and fm research ask, stubbed in tests (the lockout stub); on failure gen-N/frontier.md records 'unreachable: why' with its gen stamp and isn't retried until the next 3rd generation or --force. (4) seed N ≥ 2: row 1 is always the moonshot and coverage gets at most 7; a reseed rewrites the file the same way (idempotent test). (5) the live check on a real gen 2 is a follow-up task once gen 2 exists. Rejected: a mark in the file (forgeable), dropping obvious ideas (contradicts the steer), a model judging 'never asked' (no ground truth).
