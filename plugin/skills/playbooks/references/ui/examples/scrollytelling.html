What this shows (scrollytelling playbook)
  • Storyboard first: five steps, one change to the chart each; the CSS blocks [data-state] are its graphic column.
  • Works with scripts off: every step carries its own static figure, and a failed chart falls back to them.
  • Steps, not pixels: one IntersectionObserver band across the middle; no scroll or wheel handler at all.
  • State is a lookup on the step's index, so scrolling back reverses exactly, and #step-4 opened cold draws step 4.
  • Reduced motion is its own path: the chart swaps in place with a short cross-fade; only opacity ever animates.
  • Phone: the chart takes under half the screen and opaque cards scroll over it. Every number in a step is in the chart.
