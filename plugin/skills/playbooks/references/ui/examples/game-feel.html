What this shows

  • The juice table first. Every effect above is a row with its cause, amount and reduced twin; anything not in it was cut.
  • Hard caps. Shake stays under 2% of the stage's short side, one run under a second, then a rest. Flashes: two a second, counted together. Hit-stop: 200 ms a second. Sparks: 48 a burst, pooled, seeded. The panel above counts them live.
  • Hit-stop freezes the simulation, not the picture. Strikes during a freeze are buffered, never dropped. The HUD sits outside the shaking world.
  • Reduced motion keeps meaning. One settings object turns shake, squash and spark travel off; cracks, the HP number and a tint say the same thing. Gameplay is identical either way.
  • Sound is off by default, starts only after you ask, retriggers at most once per 50 ms, and every cue has a visual twin (the Cue readout, the flash, the cracks).
  • States. Playing, paused, smashed, and a sound failure each have a designed look. Controls are 44 px or larger and reachable by keyboard at 360 px.
