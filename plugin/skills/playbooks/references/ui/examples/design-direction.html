    # DESIGN.md: Paperwasp Zine Club

## Point of view
It should feel like a two-colour risograph print, for readers
opening a newsletter on the bus. It must never be mistaken for
a subscription-box app. (assumed)

## Direction
Riso zine, audacity bold.
Runner-up: Broadsheet, rejected: its rules and serifs read as
news, not as something made by hand.
Taste words: loud, handmade -> loud owns type and colour,
handmade gets motion.

## Knobs
- Type: condensed bold display, typewriter text; base 1rem; ratio 1.5.
- Colour: paper #F6F1E4, ink #14121A (16.5:1 on paper), spot pink
  #FF48B0 as fill only (ink on it 6.0:1), spot blue #0078BF for large
  type and borders (4.2:1), link #005C99 (6.2:1).
- Density: 8 px unit; section padding 48 px.
- Shape: radius 0; 2 px ink borders; hard 3 px offset shadow.
- Motion: two-frame steps, 160 ms; instant under reduced motion.

## Signature moves
1. Misregistration: the headline repeated in blue, offset 2 px.
2. Pieces rotated up to 3 degrees that straighten on hover.

## Banned
- Defaults to avoid 1, 3, 4, 7 and 12; gradients of any kind.

    What this shows

    • Interview and taste words: the translator turns a word into five knobs, and refuses empty words.
    • Direction bank: one row picked, a runner-up rejected in writing, audacity set to bold.
    • Signature moves: the blue misregistered headline and the tilted pieces are visible above.
    • Floors: pink is only ever a fill, so text keeps 4.5:1; focus, reduced motion and 360 px hold.
    Paperwasp is invented. A worked example of the design-direction playbook.
