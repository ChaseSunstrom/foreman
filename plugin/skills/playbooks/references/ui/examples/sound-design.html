    What this shows

    • Five cues, one voice. Same triangle timbre throughout. Meaning rides on contour: rising is good news, falling is a step back, repeated is a warning.
    • Off by default, started by a gesture. The audio context is created inside the click on "Sound: off". Volume starts at 50%. Autoplay refusals and missing audio get a plain message instead of silence.
    • Limits enforced in code. A 50 ms retrigger guard, at most 8 voices, seeded pitch jitter of 5% on the tap, peaks of -8 dBFS before the volume slider, and fades of 6 ms or more.
    • Every cue has a twin. A lamp with a glyph (not just colour), a log line and a polite live region. Reduced motion stops the lamp growing; it does not mute anything.
    • Measured, not assumed. The sound check reads the rendered samples and shows the numbers. A failing row says FAIL in words.
